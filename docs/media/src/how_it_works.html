How it works

From a phone on a nightstand to a number an insurer can use

On the phone

1Record

A phone lies flat and still on its charger. The collector reads the motion sensor through the night.

On the phone

2Reduce

Only averaged vibration spectra below 25 Hz are kept. No sound, no raw recording, no location trail.

On the server

3Stack

Spectra from many nights and many phones in one building are combined, weighted by quality.

On the server

4Identify

Peaks are fitted for pitch and damping. Lifts, fans, washing machines and phone quirks are thrown out.

For the buyer

5Score

An engineering model turns the measurement into a vulnerability score with a 5 to 95% range, per building.

Private by construction

Decided in the design, not bolted on.
