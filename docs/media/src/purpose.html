Who it is for

Insurers, reinsurers and catastrophe modellers, and owners of large building portfolios.

What they get

A score with its range for each building, the measured pitch and damping behind it, and an alert when a building's stiffness changes.

What it is not

Not a structural engineering assessment and not a condemnation notice. It is risk information for screening and pricing.
