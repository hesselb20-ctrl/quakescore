QuakeScore · the idea

Every building hums at its own pitch.

Phones lying still at night can pick up that hum. Its pitch, and how quickly it dies away, say how the building will move in an earthquake.

1Phones at rest

On a charger, lying flat, overnight. The motion sensor feels the building sway by thousandths of a millimetre.

2Stack the nights

One night shows only the phone's own noise. Many nights from many phones, added up, let the building's pitch rise out of it.

3Score with a range

The measurement updates an engineering model of that building type: a vulnerability score with its uncertainty, sold to insurers as data.
