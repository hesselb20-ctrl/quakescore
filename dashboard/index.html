    What this is and is not

    QuakeScore is a statistical screening layer. Phones lying still inside a building record its ambient vibration. Many weak sessions are stacked to estimate the building's natural frequencies and damping, which are compared with what is expected for its structural class. The result is a score from 0 to 100, where 100 is the least vulnerable, always shown with a 5-95% uncertainty band.

    It is not a structural engineering assessment. The fragility mapping is physics-based and has not been calibrated against observed damage. A score cannot tell you whether a particular building is safe. Only a licensed structural engineer who inspects the building can. If you are concerned about a building, ask its owner or manager whether a seismic evaluation exists, or contact your local building department.

    Privacy. Only a building id and spectral summaries leave the phone; no audio, no location, no content above 25 Hz. Results for a building are published only after at least 3 different contributors and 5 sessions; until then the map shows coverage only.

    Provenance. Every result is labelled. REAL: measured with real phones in the real building. SIM: produced by the simulator. FIXTURE: fabricated placeholder records used to build this demo before batch results exist. SIM and FIXTURE data are shown as "Simulated data".
